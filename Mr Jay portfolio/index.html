Profile

A dedicated project manager and community operator in the Web3 space. My career has been centered on building, guiding, and managing communities, helping projects stay organized, grow stronger, and deliver on their promises. I thrive where structure meets culture. On one hand, I handle task-based project management, organizing workflows, aligning teams, and ensuring every milestone gets done. On the other hand, I work hands-on with communities, moderating conversations, building trust, and keeping engagement active across platforms like Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, and X. For me, Web3 isn’t just about technology; it’s about people and coordination. A great idea means nothing if it can’t be communicated, managed, and supported by a community that believes in it. That’s where I come in, I make sure projects don’t just launch, but last.
